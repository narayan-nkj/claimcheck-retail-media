# Hackathon Judging Matrix — ClaimCheck

| Criterion | What judges must see | ClaimCheck proof artifact | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Application of Technology | Working product, AMD-powered path, usable demo, public code | AMD inference provenance, runnable demo, repository, video | AMD is only named or the demo does not run |
| Presentation | Clear problem, user, workflow, evidence, result, limitations | Short demo, PDF slides, cover image, script, reset plan | Generic AI story or unclear before/after |
| Business Value | Merchant action and measurable quality/service improvement | Claim-level gate, reduced unsafe publication risk, review workflow, metric table | Legal-compliance overclaim or no business action |
| Originality | Distinct chain of custody from copy to evidence to decision | Claim span → normalized claim → source → timestamp → verdict → reviewer | Generic text generator or opaque score |

The team must keep the story within the verified product boundary. ClaimCheck is a bounded pre-publication evidence gate, not legal advice, universal compliance, or a replacement for platform moderation or counsel.
