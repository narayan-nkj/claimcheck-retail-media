# Eligibility and AMD Gate

This gate is a hard prerequisite for claiming prize eligibility.

## Required checks

1. Confirm team registration and AMD AI Developer Program/event approval.
2. Confirm the authorized AMD execution target, account/token access, quota, model/runtime compatibility, and endpoint or notebook path.
3. Run the ClaimCheck workload on AMD. A hello-world or unrelated benchmark is insufficient.
4. Demonstrate both a supported case and a stale, conflicting, unsupported, or ambiguous case through the product path.
5. Capture sanitized target/device/runtime/model metadata, timestamps, fixture IDs, request/response hashes, and replay instructions.
6. Store proof without API keys, personal credentials, or private data.
7. Put the proof in the README, demo/video evidence, audit artifact, and submission checklist.

## Fail condition

If AMD execution cannot be reproduced or cannot be shown as part of the product path, mark the submission **not verified for AMD prize eligibility**. Continue development only with a clearly labeled fallback and escalate to organizers or mentors.
