Retail advertising moves faster
than manual compliance.

The solution

One evidence gate.
Three clear decisions.

ClaimCheck evaluates every ad copy statement against fresh merchant ground truth and configured compliance rules. Decisions are mathematically deterministic, explainable, and logged to an immutable ledger.

ALLOW

Instant Publication Clearance

All extracted factual claims—pricing, discounts, inventory levels, and delivery SLAs—are strictly corroborated by fresh merchant catalog and logistics feeds within freshness thresholds.

  • Corroborated against master price record
  • Real-time stock verified > 0 units
  • Carrier transit SLA confirmed
  • Cryptographically signed to Merkle audit stream
BLOCK

Factual Pre-Publication Rejection

Direct factual contradictions between the ad copy and authoritative ground truth cause an immediate, fail-closed block. Prevents deceptive claims from ever reaching the consumer.

  • Strikethrough price exceeds catalog regular price
  • Out-of-stock items rejected instantly
  • False delivery speed claims suppressed
  • Requires Senior Officer approval to override
REVIEW

Human Review Escalation

Feed synchronization conflicts, warehouse inventory discrepancies, or borderline promotional qualifiers are routed into the prioritized Human Review Queue with countdown SLAs.

  • Multi-warehouse feed contradiction detected
  • Expiring promotional pricing windows
  • Prioritized by campaign spend and risk
  • Re-extraction or confirmation workflow
How it works

Deterministic execution,
from copy to ledger.

Unlike generative AI wrappers that hallucinate compliance assessments, ClaimCheck separates model-assisted span extraction from deterministic, fail-closed rule verification.

01

Ingestion & Security Sanitization

Incoming ad copy text from programmatic campaigns, merchant dashboards, or API webhooks is immediately sanitized. Customer emails, credit cards, SSNs, phone numbers, and private API credentials are redacted before persistence or model inference.

02

Structured Claim Extraction on AMD ROCm™

The open-weights Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct model, accelerated by AMD ROCm 6.2 on AMD Instinct™ MI300X hardware (with CPU fallback), extracts precise character spans and classifies claims into 4 distinct commerce categories: PRICING, AVAILABILITY, SHIPPING_PROMISE, and PRODUCT_ATTRIBUTE.

03

Multi-Source Ground Truth Corroboration

The Target Product SKU is resolved against master merchant catalogs and real-time inventory feeds. Freshness intervals ensure pricing baseline, stock quantities, and carrier transit SLAs reflect actual warehouse reality rather than stale cached data.

04

Fail-Closed Deterministic Policy Evaluation

Every extracted claim is evaluated in under 1.2ms by strict mathematical rules (FTC deceptive price checks, stockout barriers, transit mismatch detection). If any mandatory claim fails or evidence is missing, the system fails closed to protect the platform.

05

Forward-Chained SHA-256 Tamper-Evident Ledger

Every check verdict, operator override, and re-evaluation is committed into a forward-chained Merkle audit stream. Each block cryptographically seals the previous block's SHA-256 digest, providing mathematical non-repudiation and 1-click bit-for-bit historical replay.

FAQ

Questions & Architecture Details

Generic LLMs suffer from probabilistic hallucinations and inconsistent rule enforcement. ClaimCheck uses the AMD ROCm-accelerated Llama-3.1 model strictly for structured claim extraction (locating character spans). The subsequent policy evaluation executes deterministic rule evaluation after model-assisted extraction, performing mathematical comparison against live merchant inventory, pricing records, and FTC compliance thresholds in under 1.2 milliseconds.

Production deployments target AMD Instinct™ MI300X accelerators running ROCm 6.2 with vLLM serving. In environments where GPU acceleration is offline or transitioning, ClaimCheck seamlessly activates an optimized CPU fallback engine without altering any API contracts or fail-closed security guarantees.

Every check result, human review action, or emergency override writes a discrete event to the audit stream. Each event calculates a SHA-256 hash that links to the previous event's chain digest. Any attempt to alter historical records breaks the cryptographic linkage, allowing legal and regulatory teams to prove exactly which ad copy was approved and what catalog evidence existed at that exact second.

Under decision rule DEC-010, the platform enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Junior Operators can triage the queue, confirm blocks, or trigger re-extractions, but are strictly forbidden by backend validation (returning 403 Forbidden) from authorizing overrides on factual BLOCKs. Only Senior Compliance Officers or Legal Counsel can authorize overrides, requiring recorded rationales.
